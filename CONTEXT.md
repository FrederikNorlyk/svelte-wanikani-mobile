# WaniKani Study

Study sessions let learners review or practice WaniKani subjects.

## Language

**Study session**:
A sequence of subjects the learner answers in review or practice mode, with a count of correct answers.

**Review**:
Study of a WaniKani assignment whose answer is submitted to WaniKani.

**Practice**:
Study of subjects with progress recorded locally rather than submitted as WaniKani reviews.

**Level-up**:
An increase in the learner's WaniKani level, celebrated immediately before returning to the ongoing study session or its completion flow.
